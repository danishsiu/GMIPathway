# FAQ Sources

The app's FAQ uses only facts already recorded in `gmi_research.md`. Source codes such as `[S1 p.9]` are defined in section 10 of that file. If a fact changes, update `app/src/main/res/values/faq.xml`, `values-ms/faq.xml` and this table together.

| # | Question | Facts used | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How do I apply to GMI? | Online portal with a New Applicant option; recommends the latest Chrome | S21 |
| 2 | When are the intakes? | Diplomas January and July; GAPP January, May and August; GUFP April and July; BELT March and October | S1 pp.9, 44, 48, 10 |
| 3 | How long do the programmes take? | Diplomas 3 years; GAPP 20 months at GMI then 6 months in Germany; GUFP 1 year; BELT 4 years | S7, S8, S16, S18, S32 |
| 4 | How much are the fees? | Malaysian tuition: diploma RM33,324, GAPP RM42,400, GUFP RM16,050, BELT RM69,000 | S1 pp.41, 46, 50, 25 |
| 5 | Can I get a loan or sponsorship? | MARA and PTPTN loans for Malaysian diploma students; other listed sponsors; one loan or sponsorship at a time; GMI does not approve loans | S14 p.3, S17, S1 p.52 |
| 6 | Does GMI have a hostel? | Optional hostel; RM900 per semester for diplomas | S1 p.41 |
| 7 | How practical is the learning? | 70% practical, 30% theory; 22 Advanced Technology Centres | S1 pp.6-8 |
| 8 | Will I learn German? | German Language 1 and 2 in diplomas; TELC German in GAPP; weekly classes in GUFP | S7, S1 pp.44, 49 |
| 9 | Are GMI's programmes accredited? | All listed on the MQR; diplomas at MQF Level 4; BELT registered under UTeM | S8, S32 |
| 10 | Can I continue to a degree at GMI? | Off-campus learning centre for UTeM's BELT; Engineering Technology diploma holders need CGPA 2.30 | S30, S1 p.22 |
| 11 | Is the Eligibility Checker result final? | The checker is a guide; GMI decides; offer conditions include a medical examination | S14, S1 |
| 12 | What if my qualification is not in the checker? | Other routes accepted for some programmes, assessed by admissions | S1, S36 (see `eligibility_rules.md`) |

## Left out on purpose

- **Operating hours:** not published anywhere found.
- **2027 intake dates:** not published, which is also why the app has no intake countdown.
- **Named partner universities after a diploma:** the pathway graphic is dated February 2019, so its logos are not presented as current partnerships.
