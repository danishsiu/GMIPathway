## How to read this document

Facts are marked by how firmly they are supported:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| (no mark) | Stated by an official GMI source, or the MQA register |
| `CONFLICT` | Official sources disagree. See section 9 for how it was resolved |
| `UNVERIFIED` | Could not be confirmed. Do not present as fact without checking |

**Rule used when sources disagree:** the official GMI website is preferred, as the brief asks. The exception is where a website page is shown to be out of date, for example when it carries an older code than both the May 2026 Programme Guide and the MQA register. The register is treated as the final word on accreditation.

### Malaysian-German cooperation

GMI began as a joint venture between the Governments of Malaysia and Germany. It describes itself as the pioneer of German-style technical and vocational education and training (TVET) in Malaysia. [S1 p.2, S3]

### German-based education approach

- **70% practical, 30% theory** is GMI's stated TVET learning approach. [S1 p.8]
- **22 Advanced Technology Centres**, several partnered with industry names such as ZEISS, ARBURG, DMG MORI, Emerson, SPINNER, Cisco, Bosch Rexroth and Festo. [S1 pp.6-8]
- **German language** is part of the diploma curriculum (German Language 1 and 2) and of GUFP (weekly classes). [S7, S1 p.49]
- **Industry links:** an Industry Advisory Council of industry partners. [S1 p.4]
- **Examination centre** for Cambridge A-Level, TELC and IELTS. [S2]

### Vision and mission `CONFLICT` (C3)

### Accreditation

## 7. Financial assistance

- Financial assistance is for Malaysian students only. [S17]
- GMI offers education loans through **MARA** and **PTPTN**, for Malaysian citizens at Diploma level. [S14 p.3]
- Only one loan or sponsorship is allowed at a time. Being eligible for GMI does not guarantee a loan. GMI does not approve loans. [S14 p.3, S17]
- MARA Tertiary Education Sponsorship (TESP): study fee and RM730 a month allowance for parents' income of RM12,000 a month or below; study fee only for RM12,001 to RM20,000. [S17]
- Also listed: Employees Provident Fund (EPF), Yayasan Negeri, state religious councils and zakat bodies, JAKOA, Affin Education Financing-i and Yayasan Bank Rakyat. [S1 p.52, S17]
